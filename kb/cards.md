# Debit and Credit Card Policy

## Lost or stolen card
If a card is lost or stolen, the customer should freeze it immediately through the app, net banking or the 24x7 helpline. A temporary freeze can be reversed by the customer at any time. A permanent block cancels the card and a replacement is dispatched within 5 working days at no charge for the first replacement in a year.

## Suspicious transaction alerts
When the fraud system flags a transaction, the card may be temporarily frozen to protect the customer. The customer receives an SMS and app notification and can confirm or deny the transaction. Confirmed-genuine transactions unfreeze the card instantly.

## International usage
International usage is disabled by default on new cards. Customers can enable it per card, with a per-day limit, from the app. Transactions abroad attract a 3.5% foreign currency markup.
