# KYC and Anti-Money Laundering

## KYC documents
Individuals must provide proof of identity and proof of address. Accepted documents include passport, voter ID, driving licence and Aadhaar (with consent). PAN is mandatory for accounts with high-value transactions.

## Periodic re-KYC
High-risk customers are re-verified every 2 years, medium-risk every 8 years and low-risk every 10 years. Accounts with pending re-KYC may have debit restrictions.

## Cash transactions
Cash deposits of 50,000 INR and above require PAN. Splitting deposits to stay below reporting thresholds is called structuring and is monitored.

## Suspicious transaction reporting
Suspicious transactions are reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit after review by the compliance team. Staff must not inform the customer that a report has been or will be filed.
