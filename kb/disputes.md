# Disputes and Unauthorised Transactions

## Zero liability
If the customer reports an unauthorised electronic transaction within 3 working days of receiving the alert, the customer's liability is zero. Reported within 4 to 7 working days, liability is capped based on account type. Reported after 7 working days, liability is decided per the bank's board-approved policy.

## Raising a dispute
A dispute can be raised from the app, net banking, branch or helpline for any debit transaction in the last 120 days. The bank provides a dispute reference number immediately.

## Resolution timelines
The disputed amount is credited to the account as a shadow reversal within 10 working days of reporting. Final resolution is completed within 90 days.
