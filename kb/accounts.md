# Savings Accounts and Fees

## Minimum balance
Retail savings accounts require an average monthly balance of 10,000 INR in metro branches. Premium accounts require 1,00,000 INR. Shortfall charges are proportional to the shortfall, up to 500 INR per month.

## Interest
Savings balances earn 3% per annum up to 10 lakh INR and 3.5% above, credited quarterly.

## Transfers
UPI transfers are free. NEFT and RTGS through digital channels are free; branch NEFT costs 2.50 INR plus GST up to 10,000 INR.
